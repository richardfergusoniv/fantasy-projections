# 2026 draft-board freeze — 2026-08-13

This snapshot is the frozen analytical baseline for the 2026 fantasy draft
board. Further model or weighting changes require a newly versioned evaluation;
routine roster, injury, depth-chart, and source-data refreshes remain allowed.

## Release gates

- Complete pytest suite: **57 passed**.
- 2026 projection: 3,969 player-stat rows, 32 teams, no duplicate keys.
- Fantasy board: 768 player-position rows; season-total formula error below
  `6e-14`.
- Every QB room allocates 17 game-equivalents; four rooms reconcile upward and
  28 downward. Raw availability remains in `projected_games_raw`.
- Maximum QB attempt rate: 42.0/game. Team attempt identity error below
  `2e-13`; no meaningful unmodeled QB attempts remain.
- Leakage-safe 2024→2025 fantasy evaluation: 755 frozen Week-1 contracted
  players, 166 rookies, 661 complete forecasts, 94 explicit missing forecasts,
  166 zero-game outcomes, and 215 zero-point outcomes.
- The model beats carry-forward and availability-adjusted baselines on
  Spearman correlation and season-point MAE at QB, RB, WR, and TE.
- It does **not** beat them everywhere, and the exceptions are on the
  draft-relevant metrics rather than the aggregate ones. Top-tier hits: QB
  6/12 against carry-forward's 7/12, and WR 22/36 tied with both baselines;
  the model wins only RB (15 v 14) and TE (5 v 3). VORP MAE at TE is worse
  than both baselines (39.28 against 31.45 and 32.18). So the defensible
  claim is that the model orders a whole position board better than the
  baselines - decisively, with Spearman margins of .08-.15 - not that it
  identifies the top tier better. Anyone using these numbers as the bar for
  a future change should hold it to the tier and VORP columns too, since
  those are where the current build is weakest.

## Known limits accepted for freeze

- The historical universe is the earliest regular-season Week-1 roster, not
  the full August camp population.
- Historical curated roles, target-year coordinator context, and exact elite
  correction parity are unavailable in the fantasy holdout.
- Only Seydou Traore retains an explicitly warned unresolved rookie ID.
- Sleeper remains a rough season-total comparison, not the optimization target.
