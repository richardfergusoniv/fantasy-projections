# Las Vegas Raiders Coach-Speak Sentiment Summary

**Scope:** Players mentioned in the Raiders Coachspeak Index Discord channel from February 17 through August 24, 2026.

## Core fantasy signals

- **Ashton Jeanty — elite positive:** Klint Kubiak describes him as a three-down back and a major building block, with an explicit desire to keep the best player on the field and create touches for him. A capable RB2 is still desired, implying some workload sharing rather than an every-snap role.
- **Brock Bowers — elite positive:** Staff language frames Bowers as potentially one of the league’s best receivers and a standard-bearer. The stated offensive objective is to maximize touches for Bowers and Jeanty.
- **Tre Tucker — very positive:** Repeated praise for toughness, availability, leadership, speed, and playmaking makes him the most secure Raiders WR based on staff commentary.

## Quarterbacks

- **Kirk Cousins — positive, immediate favorite:** Excellent spring, first-team work, and designation as the starter entering camp give him the current QB1 edge.
- **Fernando Mendoza — very positive development:** Coaches praise preparation, intelligence, leadership, command, and improvement, while also noting he needs reps, game exposure, and technical cleanup. Best viewed as a high-upside dynasty asset rather than a secure 2026 starter.
- **Aidan O’Connell — positive depth signal:** Strong reviews of his offseason, leadership, intelligence, and playmaking, but he remains behind Cousins and Mendoza in practical priority.

## Running backs and tight ends

- **Mike Washington — rising positive:** He is earning opportunities through practice and preseason performance, though protection and all-around refinement remain development points. He is the most important contingent back behind Jeanty.
- **Michael Mayer — very positive real-football role:** Consistently open, reliable hands, unselfish blocking, and emerging leadership are praised. Bowers limits Mayer’s fantasy target ceiling.
- **Dylan Laube and Chris Collier — neutral/open competition:** Both were named among backs competing for roles behind Jeanty without a distinct individual endorsement.
- **Zack Kuntz — cautious:** Good approach noted, but the staff says he has a long way to go, particularly in scheme knowledge and run-game value.

## Wide receivers

- **Jalen Nailor — positive but volatile:** Strong practice reviews, expectations for major contributions, and starter usage in preseason.
- **Jack Bech — positive development:** Praised for second-year growth, versatility, effort, blocking, contested catches, and preseason play.
- **Malik Benson — positive developmental:** Speed, deep-play ability, competitiveness, special-teams value, and occasional first-team reps stand out; he remains raw.
- **Dont’e Thornton — mixed/health-limited:** The staff remains optimistic about upside and effort, but injuries interrupted camp availability.
- **WR room overall — cautious-to-negative:** Kubiak said the group regressed after joint practices/preseason action and needs reliable, accountable contributors. Individual optimism does not yet equal dependable weekly projection.

## Additional context

- **Maxx Crosby — strongly positive organizational sentiment:** Leadership, coachability, and winning mentality praised; not a fantasy signal.
- **Kolton Miller — positive:** Veteran mentality praised; supportive context for offensive-line and run-game stability.
- **Geno Smith — noncommittal/obsolete roster signal:** February language framed him only as one quarterback option before the Cousins-Mendoza-O’Connell discussion took over.

## Actionable ranking

1. Prioritize Jeanty and Bowers; coach comments identify both as offensive engines.
2. Treat Tucker as the safest current WR investment.
3. Target Washington as the leading contingency RB.
4. Consider Nailor, Bech, and Benson speculative/volatile due to the room-wide concern.
5. Prefer Mendoza in dynasty; Cousins remains the near-term QB favorite.
