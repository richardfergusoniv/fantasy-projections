# Cincinnati Bengals Player Sentiment

### Executive take
Coaching and personnel comments were most favorable for Chase Brown, Tee Higgins, and Joe Burrow. Erick All Jr. drew unusually strong upside language, but his recovery and workload ramp remain material qualifiers. The actionable uncertainty is concentrated at WR3 and in the tight-end rotation.

- Prioritize All as the highest-upside TE stash, while discounting near-term certainty.
