# New Orleans Saints: Fantasy Sentiment Update

**As of August 24, 2026**

## Executive read

The Saints’ recent coachspeak continues to support Tyler Shough, Travis Etienne, Chris Olave, Jordyn Tyson, Devaughn Vele, and Juwan Johnson. The newest update materially worsens the short-term fantasy outlook for Devin Neal and Ty Chandler due to injury, gives Zamir White a meaningful audition and contingent-value boost, and mildly downgrades Oscar Delp.

## Backfield implications

1. Travis Etienne remains the clearest Saints RB target because he combines the strongest coaching praise with the best scheme/role outlook.
2. Alvin Kamara receives a modest short-term security boost from depth injuries, but not enough to remove season-long workload and organizational uncertainty.
3. Zamir White is the largest riser. He is a speculative late-round or waiver watch candidate whose preseason-finale usage matters.
4. Audric Estime retains contingent value, though White adds competition.
5. Devin Neal and Ty Chandler are the major fallers due to availability risk.
