# Jacksonville Jaguars Fantasy Sentiment Summary

**Scope:** Coachspeak Index Discord channel, player mentions since February 17, 2026. This file summarizes the previously reviewed channel findings; it is a sentiment read, not a projection model.

## Top positive signals

- **Bhayshul Tuten — very positive:** Coaches highlighted his processing, protection, receiving development, downhill running, creativity, and a strong spring. He has the clearest RB upside signal, though Jacksonville has described the backfield as a competition/committee.
- **Parker Washington — very positive:** Repeated trust and versatility endorsements: inside/outside deployment, route-tree breadth, screens/jet sweeps/YAC, and strong Trevor Lawrence rapport. Best value-riser in the receiver room.
- **Trevor Lawrence — very positive:** Praised for command, decisions, controlled aggression, spring ball security, downfield connection with Brian Thomas Jr., and connection with Washington. Passing-efficiency and rushing-use upside.
- **Brian Thomas Jr. — positive:** Better Lawrence chemistry and intentional use were emphasized. The staff also indicated a distributed-target offense, which caps certainty around elite volume.

## Role and workload risks

- **Travis Hunter — positive but volatile:** Team expects two-way use and potentially a larger cornerback share. Athletic upside is clear, but offensive snap count and weekly target reliability remain uncertain.
- **Chris Rodriguez — positive, committee-bound:** Praised for contact balance, vision, physicality, efficiency, and system familiarity. Fits as a short-yardage/inside-run candidate rather than a clear lead-back declaration.
- **LeQuint Allen — positive:** Strong comments on toughness, pass protection, third-down value, and broader passing-game use. Profiles as a complementary role player.
- **Ameer Abdullah / DeeJay Dallas / J’Mari Taylor — depth signals:** Abdullah brings veteran receiving value; Dallas is a competitive/depth option; Taylor earned developmental praise for versatility and tackle-breaking.

## Pass catchers

- **Brenton Strange — positive:** Treated as an important offensive piece, with more TE depth potentially allowing more flexible use.
- **Tanner Koziol — positive developmental:** Size, catch ability, red-zone flashes, and QB chemistry stand out; role remains unproven.
- **Nate Boerkircher — positive developmental:** Maturity and roster/formation utility were praised, without a clear fantasy workload case.
- **Jakobi Meyers — positive:** Part of the trusted receiver chemistry group, though the channel provided fewer current usage-specific notes than for Washington or Thomas.
- **CJ Williams / Josh Cameron — developmental positives:** Reliable/open-field and toughness/ball-skills praise, respectively; both remain depth-chart watches.

## Fantasy takeaways

1. Target **Tuten** for upside, but discount for committee and goal-line competition.
2. **Washington** has the strongest favorable sentiment-to-cost profile.
3. **Lawrence** is a buy; **Thomas** is a cautious buy because target concentration may be limited.
4. Treat **Hunter** as a high-variance bet until offensive-versus-defensive usage is established.
5. **Strange** is the safer late TE; **Koziol** is a speculative red-zone/waiver option.
