# Miami Dolphins Player Sentiment Summary
## Coachspeak Index — mentions since February 17, 2026

### Executive view
Dolphins commentary is most constructive on De’Von Achane, Malik Willis, Malik Washington, Caleb Douglas, Jaylen Wright, and Greg Dulcich. The largest risks are role competition, developmental uncertainty, and availability—especially for Chris Bell and Ollie Gordon.

### Strong positive sentiment
- **De’Von Achane:** Clear centerpiece. Described as a rare three-down difference-maker and building block, with expected involvement as runner and receiver. Premium fantasy priority; watch workload/ramp-up.
- **Malik Willis:** Strong upward trajectory in confidence, command, consistency, pocket operation, mobility, and leadership. First-team opportunity supports Superflex/2QB upside, though development volatility remains.
- **Malik Washington:** Breakout-friendly feedback on route development, catch-and-run ability, consistency, and rapport with Willis. Strong late-round receiver target if role holds.
- **Caleb Douglas:** Rookie starter-caliber signals: versatility, physical gifts, difficult catches, blocking, and consistency. Stash with a real early-snap path, but a developing player.
- **Jaylen Wright:** Coaches praise speed, power, finishing, scheme fit, and practice habits. Strong contingent-upside RB; RB2 competition remains unsettled.
- **Greg Dulcich:** Positive camp and leap-candidate language, with a pass-game rapport signal. Health/status uncertainty limits confidence.

### Positive but role-dependent
- **Quinn Ewers:** Processing, timing, and recognition improved, but preseason mistakes and Willis’s lead status cap immediate value. Deep Superflex developmental stash.
- **Ollie Gordon:** Every-down traits, size, and receiving/protection progress are encouraging. Discipline criticism and rib issue add risk; handcuff/short-yardage upside.
- **Chris Bell:** Size, speed, explosiveness, and physicality are praised, but rehab/non-contact timeline makes him a longer-term stash.
- **Jalen Tolbert:** Reliable, physical, and dependable. Useful depth signal without clear target-dominance evidence.
- **Tutu Atwell:** Consistent and assignment-sound; more depth-chart than standalone fantasy appeal.
- **Jalen Reagor:** Earned a preseason WR3 chance via effort and blocking, but coaches expect weekly rotation/volatility.
- **Kevin Coleman:** Explosive and versatile, with return-game value; developmental depth.
- **Seydou Traore:** Athletic mismatch traits but raw; long-term dynasty dart.
- **Will Kacmarek:** Elite blocking and useful receiving ability; supports the run game more than fantasy lineups.

### Negative or unresolved
- **Tua Tagovailoa:** Organizational uncertainty, including trade possibility earlier in the cycle, while staff attention has favored Willis/Ewers. Avoid assuming a meaningful Dolphins role absent a concrete change.
- **Austin Jackson:** Day-to-day health topic rather than a fantasy asset; his availability is supportive of Miami’s rushing environment.

### Fantasy actions
- **Prioritize:** Achane; Willis in Superflex/2QB; Malik Washington; Wright.
- **Upside stashes:** Douglas, Dulcich, Gordon, Chris Bell.
- **Monitor, don’t force:** Tolbert, Atwell, Reagor, Coleman, Traore, Ewers.
- **Avoid pending clarity:** Tua.
