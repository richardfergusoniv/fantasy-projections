# Player Sentiment Analysis — August 31, 2026

## Scope and method

This report analyzes every football player mentioned in the available August 31 Discord search results from *The Coachspeak Index*. Sentiment is based on the quoted coach/front-office language, with separate consideration for:

- Direct performance or role endorsement
- Workload and deployment implications
- Health/availability language
- Whether praise is actionable for fantasy purposes

A positive quote is not automatically a fantasy upgrade: committee language, unclear roles, and injury-management caveats are reflected in the interpretation.
