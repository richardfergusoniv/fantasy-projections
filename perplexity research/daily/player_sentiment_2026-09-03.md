# September 3, 2026 Player Sentiment

Source: The Coachspeak Index Discord `#general`, 9/3/26 search results.

## Executive summary

The strongest positive player signals were for **Michael Wilson**, **Roschon Johnson**, **Drake Maye**, **Caleb Williams**, **Bryce Lance**, **Barion Brown**, **Nico Collins**, and **Jonah Coleman**. The strongest negative role signal was **Anthony Richardson** being explicitly designated as the backup quarterback. **Chris Holani** and **Kyle Monangai** carried more conditional/fragile outlooks, while **Zavion Thomas** was a talent-positive but role-uncertain player.
