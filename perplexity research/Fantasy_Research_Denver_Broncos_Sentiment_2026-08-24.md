# Denver Broncos Fantasy Sentiment Research

**Scope:** Coach-speak and team-context signals since February 17, 2026, including Sean Payton comments supplied on August 24, 2026.

## Executive takeaway
Denver’s strongest positive fantasy sentiment belongs to Pat Bryant, Jonah Coleman, Jaylen Waddle, Marvin Mims, and Evan Engram. The central risk remains role distribution: the Broncos have several viable backs and receivers, creating uncertainty around weekly volume even where talent and coach praise are strong.

## August 24 update

### Jonah Coleman
The new comments reinforce Coleman’s standing as the back with the strongest rookie-readiness signal. “Savvy,” “smart,” good contact balance, and no apparent rookie adjustment burden point toward broad trust and a realistic path to increased work. This raises his probability of early meaningful snaps but does not eliminate committee risk.

### RJ Harvey
The update improves Harvey’s PPR outlook specifically. Payton identified his receiving ability as a genuine strength and said he is having a good camp in both rushing and receiving. Raise his target/third-down role expectation; do not assume he controls early-down volume.

### Pat Bryant
Bryant receives the clearest upgrade. Payton’s detailed description of his catch consistency, hands, yards-after-catch mechanics, intelligence, suddenness, and size is strong evidence of translatable role trust. Bryant is the most attractive Denver WR riser in late-round and dynasty formats, while still facing target competition.

## Draft implications
- **Raise:** Pat Bryant, Jonah Coleman, RJ Harvey in PPR, Jaylen Waddle, Marvin Mims, Evan Engram.
- **Talent plus uncertainty:** J.K. Dobbins and RJ Harvey; multiple capable runners likely cap certainty of carries.
- **Avoid fixed WR hierarchy assumptions:** Denver expects package-based usage, which can produce volatility for Bryant, Mims, and Franklin.
- **Health watch:** Dobbins has the most material camp health concern; Waddle’s issue appeared short term; Nix’s mobility was described as restored.
