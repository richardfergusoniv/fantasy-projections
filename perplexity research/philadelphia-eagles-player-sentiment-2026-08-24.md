# Philadelphia Eagles Player Sentiment
## Coachspeak Index review — Feb. 17 to Aug. 24, 2026

### Executive view
The clearest positive fantasy-coachspeak signals belong to Jalen Hurts, DeVonta Smith, and Saquon Barkley. Dontayvion Wicks and rookie TE Eli Stowers stand out as the most notable upside names, while Makai Lemon has strong praise offset by a hamstring-related availability concern.

|---|---|---|
