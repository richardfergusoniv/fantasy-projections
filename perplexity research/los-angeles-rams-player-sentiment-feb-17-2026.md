# Los Angeles Rams Player Sentiment
## Coachspeak Index Discord — Feb. 17 to Aug. 24, 2026

### Overall takeaway
Rams coach sentiment is highly favorable for the core passing game and Blake Corum. The primary fantasy uncertainty centers on Puka Nacua’s short-term health, the degree of RB workload sharing between Kyren Williams and Corum, and a deep, matchup-driven tight-end committee. The original findings are drawn from the Rams Coachspeak Index Discord channel. [page:1]
