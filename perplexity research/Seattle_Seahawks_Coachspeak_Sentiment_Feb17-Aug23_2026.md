# Fantasy Research: Seattle Seahawks Coachspeak Sentiment Summary

**Coverage:** Player mentions from Feb. 17 through Aug. 23, 2026, based on the Coachspeak Index Seahawks discussion reviewed in this project.

## Executive take
Seattle’s coachspeak is most favorable for the core passing-game pieces—Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Cooper Kupp, and Rashid Shaheed—and for rookie running back Jadarian Price. The central risk is availability: Zach Charbonnet, Tory Horton, Emanuel Wilson, Kenny McIntosh, Jake Bobo, and Robbie Ouzts carried material injury concerns.

## Actionable roster view

**Target or buy:** Rashid Shaheed, Jadarian Price, Cooper Kupp, Jaxon Smith-Njigba; Elijah Arroyo in deeper TE formats.

**Hold cautiously:** Zach Charbonnet, Tory Horton, Emanuel Wilson, and Kenny McIntosh. Their value is chiefly medical-timeline dependent.

**Deep-league watch list:** Jacardia Wright, George Holani, and Jalen Milroe.

**Avoid in standard 2026 redraft unless very cheap with IR capacity:** Jake Bobo and Robbie Ouzts.

## Team-level implications

- The Seahawks’ passing game has concentrated positive signals: JSN projects as a priority read, Shaheed as a designed-touch/deep-threat expansion candidate, and Kupp as a trusted high-leverage contributor.
- The RB room reads as an open competition rather than a declared hierarchy. Price has the strongest current upward momentum, while Charbonnet has coach trust but unresolved availability.
- Injuries to Horton and Bobo increase the practical importance of Seattle’s established top receiving trio rather than clearly identifying a reliable depth replacement.
