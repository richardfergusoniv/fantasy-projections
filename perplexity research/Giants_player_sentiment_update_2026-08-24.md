# New York Giants Player Sentiment Update

**Date:** August 24, 2026  
**Scope:** Fantasy Research / Coachspeak notes through the Aug. 24 Harbaugh update.

## Executive takeaways

- **Malik Nabers:** Meaningfully improved Week 1 outlook. He reportedly moved better than at any prior point in rehab, had a good practice, and removed the red non-contact jersey. The coach would not formally guarantee Week 1, so projected early-season workload still carries some uncertainty.
- **Jaxson Dart:** Strongly positive sentiment remains intact. Staff cites exceptional arm talent, off-platform ability, vision, pocket feel, fundamental work, and tremendous upside.
- **Cam Skattebo and Tyrone Tracy:** The staff has consistently signaled a meaningful two-back deployment. Skattebo has returned to full activity and is viewed as a top-tier, physical runner; Tracy is expected to play extensively as well.
- **Isaiah Likely and Theo Johnson:** The offense projects to use two tight ends often, with significant passing-game involvement. Johnson had a practice collision on Aug. 24 but was initially thought to be okay.
- **Calvin Austin:** Upgraded as a depth/value receiver. The staff emphasizes his three-position flexibility, reliability, route-running, and catch ability.

## Aug. 24 update

### Malik Nabers

- Reportedly looked better moving than at any prior point in his recovery.
- Had a good practice and removed the red non-contact jersey.
- The staff says he is strong, in a good place, and doing very well in rehab.
- Harbaugh would not make a direct Week 1 declaration, though he acknowledged that a positive inference is reasonable.

**Fantasy impact:** Upgrade Nabers’s availability outlook. Treat the removal of the red jersey as a meaningful return-to-contact progression point, not a guarantee of an unrestricted Week 1 snap share. Maintain a modest risk discount until normal practice participation and a clear workload signal are confirmed.

### Theo Johnson

- Sustained a collision in practice.
- The initial comment was that he was likely okay, with evaluation to follow.

**Fantasy impact:** No material projection change until subsequent practice/injury information is available.

### Calvin Austin

- Praised for three-position versatility, including outside-receiver ability despite a smaller frame.
- Called reliable, a correct route-runner, and a dependable pass catcher.

**Fantasy impact:** Upgrade as a late-round/deep-roster and best-ball option. His versatility creates a credible path to offensive snaps, although target-share certainty remains low.

### Jaxson Dart

- Praised for exceptional arm talent, off-platform throws, field vision, pocket awareness, extensive fundamentals work, and tremendous upside.

**Fantasy impact:** Reinforces a high-ceiling quarterback projection. The comments validate development and talent, though they do not independently establish a specific passing-volume projection.

### Offensive line

- Jermaine Eluemunor was expected back the next day.
- Francis Mauigoa was also expected back the next day.

**Fantasy impact:** A modest positive for the overall offense, pass protection, and run-game continuity; not a direct player-projection change.

## Draft and roster notes

- Prioritize Dart in superflex/2QB formats and Likely as a high-upside tight end.
- Draft Nabers as an elite talent with a decreasing—but still present—early-season health/workload risk.
- Price Skattebo as the lead power/upside back, but do not ignore Tracy’s stated role in the expected committee.
- Consider Fields in dynasty and deeper redraft formats; consider Austin in best ball and deep benches.
- Do not overreact to the Johnson collision unless later reporting identifies an actual limitation.
