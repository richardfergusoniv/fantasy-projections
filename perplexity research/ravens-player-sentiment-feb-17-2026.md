# Baltimore Ravens Player Sentiment

**Coverage:** February 17–August 22, 2026  
**Source:** The Coachspeak Index Ravens Discord channel

## Executive Take

The Ravens’ most favorable coachspeak signals center on **Lamar Jackson, Derrick Henry, Zay Flowers, Mark Andrews, and rookie Ja’Kobi Lane**. The most material concerns are **Rashod Bateman’s off-field uncertainty**, **Rasheen Ali’s undefined health issue**, and **Danny Pinter’s anticipated extended absence**.

## Fantasy Read

### Core targets

- Lamar Jackson remains the offense’s centerpiece and offers the clearest high-end quarterback case.
- Derrick Henry projects for another heavily featured workload.
- Zay Flowers has the strongest receiver-usage signal among established players.
- Mark Andrews remains a core passing-game option with rebound potential.

### Best upside bets

- Ja’Kobi Lane has the strongest positive momentum of the non-established players, especially for red-zone and outside-receiver work.
- Rashod Bateman offers opportunity-based upside but should be discounted for legal and potential availability risk.
- Elijah Sarratt, Devontez Walker, and LaJohntay Wester are deeper-league watch-list candidates rather than secure redraft targets.

### Monitor closely

- Bateman’s legal and disciplinary status.
- Rasheen Ali’s health and practice participation.
- Lane’s preseason and early regular-season snap share.
- Whether Flowers’ promised expanded deployment translates into targets, carries, and red-zone work.
