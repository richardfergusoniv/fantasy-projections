# Arizona Cardinals Fantasy Sentiment Summary

**Scope:** Player sentiment drawn from Cardinals coach/GM comments since February 17, 2026, updated through August 24, 2026.

## Executive view

- **Trey McBride** has the strongest positive sentiment. Coaches view him as a central offensive building block and have discussed expanding how he is deployed.
- **Marvin Harrison Jr.** remains strongly positive: intelligence, process, growing confidence, and flexibility to play inside or outside are recurring themes.
- **Michael Wilson** is the clearest rising receiver. Coaches describe him as tough, cerebral, versatile, and still ascending.
- The running-back room is talented but the coaching staff has repeatedly described a committee-based approach rather than committing to a workhorse.

## August 24 update

### Jeremiyah Love

LaFleur said Love is progressing and in the “next phase,” but he will not participate in any practice activity for now. This changes Love from a positive talent thesis with a minor caveat to a positive long-term thesis with material early-season availability risk.

### Trey Benson

LaFleur declined to establish a timeline and said the team would know more after the next game; Benson was then released as waived/injured. His Cardinals value is eliminated. His exit improves the eventual structural path to touches for Love and marginally improves the early opportunity for Conner and Allgeier.

### Carson Beck

Beck’s throwing session was positive, but Friday remained uncertain. LaFleur emphasized that missed practice materially slows football development, so Beck remains a longer-term development play rather than an immediate challenge to Brissett.

## Actionable implications

1. Prioritize McBride as the clearest offensive centerpiece.
2. Maintain strong confidence in Harrison and upgrade Wilson as a value/rising-role candidate.
3. Treat the backfield as a committee: Conner and Allgeier receive the most practical short-term boost while Love is sidelined.
4. Preserve Love’s high-upside outlook but discount early-season availability until he resumes practice.
5. Remove Benson from Cardinals projections.
